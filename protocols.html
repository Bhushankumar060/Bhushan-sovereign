PRACTICAL SYSTEMS

PROTOCOLS

A repository of practical, repeatable operating procedures tested in the field. These are not dogma or theoretical guru rules, but honest behavioral blueprints.

COMMUNICATION // PROTOCOL 01 VER 1.2 · REVISED AUG 2026

Daily Articulation & Synthesis

A systematic method for stripping verbal clutter, reducing conversational latency, and articulating complex concepts under stress.

Core Axioms
  • Lead with the definitive thesis in the first 10 seconds.
  • Eliminate defensive filler phrases ('sort of', 'maybe', 'I guess').
  • Embrace clean silence rather than using vocal drag ('um', 'uh', 'like').
01 / The 3-Min Voice Memo
Record a continuous 180s monologue on a concept learned today. Review at 1.0x and log all filler words.
02 / 25-Word Reduction
Distill that exact concept into a single 25-word summary using active verbs only.
03 / Stress-Test Delivery
Recite the sentence without notes while maintaining unbroken eye contact with camera or mirror.
DISCIPLINE // PROTOCOL 02 VER 2.0 · ACTIVE STANDARD

The Zero-Day & Friction Elimination

Removing decision latency so habit execution becomes mechanically inevitable, independent of mood or motivation.

Core Axioms
  • Never allow a zero day in the core anchor habit.
  • Design the room so starting friction equals zero.
  • Never negotiate with yourself in the moment of action; execute pre-determined schedules.
01 / Evening Stage Setting
At 21:30, place all required books, training attire, and workspaces in plain sight.
02 / 5-Second Initiation
Count down 5-4-3-2-1 and initiate physical movement immediately before mental resistance starts.
03 / Baseline Floor Threshold
On days of extreme fatigue, execute the 10-minute non-negotiable minimum to preserve the psychological streak.
FITNESS // PROTOCOL 03 VER 1.4 · BIOMECHANICAL FOUNDATION

Foundational Strength & Metabolic Capacity

Coaching-grade principles for developing high physical capability and neurological stability to fuel intellectual drive.

Core Axioms
  • Compound barbell and bodyweight movements precede cosmetic isolation work.
  • Track load, rep quality, and bar speed objectively in a physical logbook.
  • Zone 2 cardiovascular base is mandatory for nervous system recovery.
01 / Movement Prep (10m)
Thoracic spine rotation, hip flexor activation, and dynamic joint lubrication.
02 / Heavy Anchor Pattern
1 primary compound movement (Squat, Hinge, Press, or Pull) performed at RPE 7.5–8.5.
03 / Zone 2 Conditioning
30–45 minutes of steady aerobic work maintaining pure nasal breathing twice weekly.
DIGITAL // PROTOCOL 04 VER 1.1 · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

High-Signal Curation & Output Distillation

Treating modern computing and AI as personal intellectual leverage without falling prey to algorithmic distraction traps.

Core Axioms
  • Strict ratio of 1 hour of synthesis/output for every 1 hour of research input.
  • No passive feed consumption on mobile devices before noon.
  • Publish field-tested learnings in public to create an unforgeable track record.
01 / Morning Attention Lock
First 3 hours of the day dedicated to deep study, writing, and training with zero social media access.
02 / AI Sparring Session
Subject drafts and hypotheses to critical AI counter-arguments to identify cognitive flaws.
03 / Weekly Public Distillation
Publish one transparent field artifact or journal entry summarizing lessons from the week.
